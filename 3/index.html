U.S. Parks & Outdoor Destinations

Explore fun and unique parks across the United States.

Topic Description

Parks and outdoor destinations give people from the U.S. and outside of the U.S. opportunities to travel, and participate in different recreational activites. For this project, I chose to focus on parks throughout the United States, specifically theme parks, water parks, wildlife parks, and national parks. I chose this because of how fond I am of different parks and exploring nature while still have as much fun as possible.

There are already websites that help people learn about different parks and attractions. For example, The National Park Service website provides information about national parks. Another example is the Great Rivers Routes website that provides information about outdoor recreation in parts of Illinois, including hiking, trails, eagle watching, parks, and other attractions. While this website is useful for finding different types of recreational activities within a specific state, my application will focus specifically on parks across the United States. This will give users one place to compare and discover different park destinations throughout the country.

My application will help users explore different types of parks from one website. Users will first select the type of park they are interested in and then choose a specific destination. The application will then provide useful information about that destination. I want users to be able to get information about the type of park they would like to visit based on their preference with a large range of selections.

Application Description

Page 1: Home

The Home page will provide a brief overview of the four types of parks featured on the website: theme parks, water parks, wildlife parks, and national parks. The page will also include one image related to the different park categories and a link that directs users to the Explore Parks page.

Page 2: Explore Parks

The Explore Parks page will be the main interactive part of the application. Users will complete two steps to explore a park. First, they will select a type of park. After selecting a park type, they will choose one of four destinations within that category.

Step 1: Select a Park Type

  1. Theme Parks
  2. Water Parks
  3. Wildlife Parks
  4. National Parks

Theme Parks

If the user selects Theme Parks, they will be able to choose from the following destinations:

  • Walt Disney World - Florida
  • Universal Orlando Resort - Florida
  • Dollywood - Tennessee
  • Six Flags Over Texas - Texas

Water Parks

If the user selects Water Parks, they will be able to choose from the following destinations:

  • Universal Volcano Bay - Florida
  • Aquatica Orlando - Florida
  • Schlitterbahn New Braunfels - Texas
  • Noah's Ark Waterpark - Wisconsin

Wildlife Parks

If the user selects Wildlife Parks, they will be able to choose from the following destinations:

  • San Diego Zoo Safari Park - California
  • Disney's Animal Kingdom - Florida
  • Yellowstone Bear World - Idaho
  • Lion Country Safari - Florida

National Parks

If the user selects National Parks, they will be able to choose from the following destinations:

  • Yellowstone National Park - Wyoming, Montana, and Idaho
  • Yosemite National Park - California
  • Grand Canyon National Park - Arizona
  • Great Smoky Mountains National Park - Tennessee and North Carolina

Final Destination Information

After the user selects a specific park, the application will display information about that destination.

  1. Park name
  2. Location
  3. Short description
  4. Top three things to do
  5. Best time of the year to visit
  6. Image of the destination

Page 3: Park Planning Guide

The Park Planning Guide will provide additional information for people planning to visit outdoor destinations. It will provide information that users can read before planning a trip. The guide will include information about planning a park visit, what visitors may want to bring, and outdoor safety.

Page 4: Contact Us & Suggest a Park

The Contact Us page will eventually allow users to contact the website or recommend another park that could be added. The form will collect the user's name, email address, and a message. Users who want to suggest a destination will also be able to provide the name of the park and its park type.
