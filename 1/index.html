Princess Tiana

About Tiana

Tiana is a character from the movie The Princess and the Frog. She is hardworking and determined to achieve her dream of owning her own restaurant. In that process, she got turned into a frog , and went on a magical adventure with Prince Naveen.

Why I Like Princess Tiana

  • I like Tiana because she works hard to accomplish her goals.
  • I like Tiana because her movie came out on my 5th birthday, and I had a Tiana theme birthday party .
  • I like that she was the first Black Disney princess which gave me a lot of representation as a little girl.
