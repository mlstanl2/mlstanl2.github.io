About Grand Canyon National Park

Grand Canyon National Park is known for its enormous canyon, scenic overlooks, hiking trails, and colorful rock formations.

Location

Arizona

Top Things to Do

  1. Visit scenic overlooks.
  2. Explore hiking trails.
  3. Watch the sunrise or sunset.

Best Time to Visit

Spring and fall are popular because temperatures are generally more comfortable for outdoor activities.
