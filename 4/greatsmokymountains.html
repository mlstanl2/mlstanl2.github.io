About Great Smoky Mountains National Park

Great Smoky Mountains National Park is known for its mountains, forests, wildlife, hiking trails, and scenic views.

Location

Tennessee and North Carolina

Top Things to Do

  1. Explore hiking trails.
  2. Look for wildlife.
  3. Visit scenic overlooks.

Best Time to Visit

Spring and fall are popular because visitors can experience wildflowers or colorful fall foliage.
