About Universal Volcano Bay

Universal Volcano Bay is a tropical-themed water park featuring water slides, pools, rivers, and other water attractions.

Location

Orlando, Florida

Top Things to Do

  1. Experience the water slides.
  2. Relax in the lazy river.
  3. Enjoy the pools and water attractions.

Best Time to Visit

Late spring and summer are popular times for enjoying the water attractions and warm weather.
