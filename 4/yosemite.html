About Yosemite National Park

Yosemite National Park is known for its mountains, waterfalls, valleys, forests, hiking trails, and scenic views.

Location

California

Top Things to Do

  1. See Yosemite's waterfalls.
  2. Explore hiking trails.
  3. Visit scenic overlooks.

Best Time to Visit

Spring is popular for waterfalls, while summer provides access to many areas of the park.
