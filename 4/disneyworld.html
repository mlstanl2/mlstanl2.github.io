About Walt Disney World

Walt Disney World is a large theme park resort located in Florida. Visitors can experience rides, themed attractions, entertainment, restaurants, and character experiences.

Location

Orlando, Florida

Top Things to Do

  1. Explore the different theme parks.
  2. Experience rides and attractions.
  3. Watch shows, parades, and entertainment.

Best Time to Visit

Spring and fall can be good times to visit because temperatures may be more comfortable than during the summer.
