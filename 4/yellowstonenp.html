About Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone National Park is known for its wildlife, geothermal features, mountains, forests, lakes, and outdoor recreation.

Location

Wyoming, Montana, and Idaho

Top Things to Do

  1. See geysers and other geothermal features.
  2. Look for wildlife.
  3. Explore hiking trails and scenic areas.

Best Time to Visit

Late spring through early fall is popular because more roads and visitor areas are accessible.
