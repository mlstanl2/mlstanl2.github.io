About Dollywood

Dollywood is a theme park featuring roller coasters, family attractions, live entertainment, food, and seasonal events.

Location

Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

Top Things to Do

  1. Ride roller coasters and other attractions.
  2. Watch live music and entertainment.
  3. Try food throughout the park.

Best Time to Visit

Spring and fall are popular times to visit because of the comfortable weather and seasonal scenery.
