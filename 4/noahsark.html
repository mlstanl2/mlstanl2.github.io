About Noah's Ark Waterpark

Noah's Ark Waterpark is an outdoor water park featuring water slides, pools, and attractions for families and thrill seekers.

Location

Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin

Top Things to Do

  1. Ride the water slides.
  2. Enjoy the wave pools.
  3. Explore family water attractions.

Best Time to Visit

Summer is a great time to visit because the outdoor attractions are open for the season.
